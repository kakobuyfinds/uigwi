Shopping guides

Sort LoloBuy finds by what you need

Keep the original seller link, listing date, product variant and colorway in one row. When a Weidian product link or Taobao listing has several versions, confirm the variant in the selection menu. For 1688 listings, check the minimum order quantity before comparing prices.

LoloBuy shoes and clothing size guide

For shoes, measure your foot and compare the result with the seller’s sneaker size chart. Ask for an insole length measurement if the chart is unclear. For a hoodie, measure the chest width and sleeve length of one you own. Compare the waist measurement and inseam for pants, and check head circumference for fitted caps.

LoloBuy QC photo checklist

Match the order variant to the warehouse photos. Ask for an outsole photo and a toe-box close-up for sneakers. For jackets, check seam alignment and zipper operation. For jewelry, request a clasp close-up and the chain length or bracelet circumference.

LoloBuy shipping cost checklist

Record domestic delivery, parcel weight and outer box dimensions before asking for an international shipping quote. Compare actual weight with volumetric weight and ask which one the carrier bills. Include any consolidation fee, tracking option and import charges shown for your destination.

Frequently asked questions

What is a LoloBuy spreadsheet?

It is a curated index of product listings. Use it to build a shortlist, then confirm the seller link, selected variant and measurements on the current listing.

Where do the category links lead?

The ten category links open the corresponding categories on HipoBuyFind. This is an independent shopping guide.

Can QC photos confirm authenticity?

QC photos can show visible details such as stitching, labels and condition. For authenticity, use a verifiable purchase record or an independent authentication service.

How do I compare international shipping quotes?

Request quotes for the same destination, parcel dimensions and weight. Compare the billed weight, tracking, delivery estimate and any taxes or fees shown at checkout.
